##### Ar Tel, Angler

> Noting Ar Tel's distress, you ask what the matter is.
> 
> "Oh, you noticed that, eh?" The angler looks at you sheepishly. "Well, there's this lutrinal I rescued from a naiad a few months ago. Usually the lutrinals know enough to avoid the areas where naiads grow, but this one was young enough that I guess he hadn't learned that yet. I couldn't just leave him there!" Ar Tel looks at you for reassurance, and you encourage Ar Tel to continue.
> 
> "So anyway, I brought him home and nursed him back to health. And at first it was great having the little guy around. But as he started to feel better, he started to get... destructive. Now my houseplants are destroyed, my furniture is all scratched up, and I swear the he knocks stuff off the table just to get a rise out of me. I haven't had a good night's sleep in weeks." You notice the bags under Ar Tel's eyes.
> 
> "I can't return him to the wild; he's imprinted on people and it would be bad for both him and the people of White Sky if he kept leading other lutrinals up here. But I can't keep him myself. Once, on a fishing trip to the High Basin I met Lyn Mira, and she said she always wanted a pet; do you think you could find her home in Branch and see if she's willing to take care of the little guy for me?"

##### Elder Murtok Gral

> "You're running errands for Zek, right?" the old man asks, his eyes still closed. He's so relaxed you'd wonder if he might be talking in his sleep, if it weren't for the fact that he couldn't know about your conversation with Zek unless he had overheard it.
> 
> He gestures vaguely towards a collection of personal affects resting in a pile on a nearby rock. "You'll find an infusion canteen among my things," he says, "the one with the ribbon tied around it. I've taken the liberty of filling it up for you already; feel free to use it on your way to the Northern Outpost. When you get there, please give it to Lunn. He looked fairly ragged the last time I saw him and I'm concerned he might not be hydrating well enough."
> 
> You don't even have a chance to respond before he seems to nod off again.

##### Vira, Lake Guide

> You manage to flag Vira down, and she closes the distance across the lake to you at incredible speed. "Hello there," she says with a tight-lipped little nod. "Did you need help getting across the lake?"
> 
> You explain that you're actually here to see if *she* needs help and she looks taken aback for a moment. "Oh, why yes I do," she replies. She reaches down into her boat and produces what appears to be a thick bundle of dolewood panels. With several deft movements, she unfolds the bundle and before your very eyes it transforms into a small canoe, which she rests on the surface of the water.
> 
> "Could you take this down to Tumbledown for me?" She asks. "You can ride it all the way there if you like. A good friend of mine—Kasende—lives down there. We don't get to see each other often enough, so I thought if she had a better way to get upriver..." she trails off.

##### Tollin Lang, Sculptor

> "I see you there," Tollin says without taking his eyes off his work. "Just one more piece..." he sticks out his tongue as he gently nudges a shiny glass shard into the perfect spot. "There," he says, leaning back and plucking his gloves off his fingers.
> 
> You tell him Zek had to leave but asked you to check in. You detect the slightest hint of disappointment cross Tollin's face before he replaces it with a jolly grin. "Ah, yes, very considerate of him to think of me! In fact, I do have something." He rummages in his nearby satchel until he produces a thick bound sketchpad.
> 
> "I've been dreaming up a piece meant to represent how we're connected to the past through both our ancestors and the Estians who lived here before us. I was hoping to get an opinion on it from Spirit Speaker Nal. Can you take these sketches to her?"

##### Elder Mora Orlin

> "What do you think you're doing with that hunk of granite?" Elder Mora Orlin snaps at a passing apprentice. "I *know* you're not taking a *silicate* rock to the *carbon* forge. Go put that back where you found it and fetch some limestone. And re-read your geological guidebook!" She rolls her eyes and shares a look with you as the apprentice scurries away. "Got to keep the young'uns on their toes," she says. "They're good kids but still have a lot to learn."
> 
> You explain that you're checking in with her on Zek's behalf. Wordlessly, she nods, and then turns her back to you and enters her nearby office. For a moment, you aren't sure if you're supposed to follow, or if she just ended the conversation. But she re-emerges and tosses a darter at you. You catch it clumsily.
> 
> "Take that to Kordo over in Lone Tree. Fixed it up for him, should shoot much straighter now," she says, turning away. Then she turns back to you, with a tiny smile. "Oh, and thanks kid."
