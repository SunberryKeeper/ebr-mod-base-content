##### Ar Tel, Angler

> It takes some effort to catch Ar Tel's attention since the angler is so wrapped up in thought. Once you do, however, the words come out in a flood.
> 
> "I'm so glad you're here," Ar Tel says, grabbing your arm and leading you to a little tide-pool where a small make-shift corral has been set up with hastily laid sticks. "I found... something while I was fishing out here, and I think it's something you rangers would be interested in."
> 
> Sure enough, in the center of the tide pool is an ugly little slug-like... thing. A biomeld, probably, given its appearance. "It's bigger now than it was even a few hours ago," Ar Tel explains. "I think we need to get this down to the biological outpost so Sirisana Mir can take a closer look at it."

Complete the **RETRIEVE THE PACKAGE (AR TEL, ANGLER)** mission. Search the general set for a Reclaimer Grub. Gain the **DELIVER THE PACKAGE (BIOLOGICAL OUTPOST)** mission and attach it to the Reclaimer Grub.
###### Travel to the Biological Outpost to deliver the package.

##### Elder Murtok Gral

> Elder Murtok Gral opens one eye and peers at you for a moment before closing it again. "How can I help you, young'un?" he asks. You explain that you're here on Zek Tal's behalf.
> 
> "Ah, good," he says, shifting his weight on his blanket slightly. "I've been looking everywhere for him." You cock an eyebrow as he continues. "I collected this fresh kelpweed this morning—" at this he pats a basket resting nearby "—because I hear they're running out of it down in Branch. I don't have to tell a successful ranger like you how important it is for healing poultices."
> 
> He slides the basket towards you. "Do you think you could take this to Tesoral for me?"

Complete the **RETRIEVE THE PACKAGE (ELDER MURTOK GRAL)** mission. Search the lakeshore set for a copy of Fresh Blue Kelpweed and put it into play within reach. Gain the **DELIVER THE PACKAGE (ELDER TESORAL HALE)** mission and attach it to the Fresh Blue Kelpweed.
###### Travel to Branch and clear Elder Tesoral Hale with <span class="progress"></span> to deliver the package.

##### Vira, Lake Guide

> Vira returns your wave from a distance and then makes her way over to you, her paddle cleanly cleaving the water in a way that looks effortless but which you know is a skill won with many years' practice.
> 
> "Can I help you?" Vira asks, setting her paddle horizontally across the bow of her longboat.
> 
> You explain that you're here on Zek's request, and she brightens a little. "Oh, that's good! I thought I was going to have to eat all of these myself," she says, a little wry smile on her face as she produces a small green food pouch. "Have you met a young girl wandering the Valley by the name of Quisi? About this tall, has lots of questions?" You nod. You're familiar with the youth.
> 
> "Well, I managed to get ahold of her favorite snack," Vira replies, handing you the bag. "Do you think you could find her and give her some? Have a few yourself, too, for your trouble. If you don't know where to look, check Crossroads Station; someone there is bound to have seen her recently."

Complete the **RETRIEVE THE PACKAGE (VIRA, LAKE GUIDE)** mission. Search the Rewards set for a copy of Quisi's Favorite Snack and put it into play. If one cannot be found, search each ranger's deck, hand, and discard pile for one instead. Gain the **DELIVER THE PACKAGE (QUISI VOS, RASCAL)** mission and attach it to a copy of Quisi's Favorite Snack in play.
###### Find Quisi Vos, Rascal and clear her with <span class="progress"></span> to deliver the package.

##### Tollin Lang, Sculptor

> You carefully position yourself where you believe Tollin can see you, but as the minutes pass, you realize he's just too absorbed in his work to notice that you're there. You quietly clear your throat, and Tollin jumps, startled, causing a small cascade of flashing glass pieces to fly everywhere. He looks upset for just a moment, and then he laughs and sighs, turning to face you directly.
> 
> "You wouldn't believe how often I do that," he says, a little sheepishly, brushing some glass off his pants. "What is it that you need?"
> 
> You mention Zek's request to see if he needs anything delivered. "Oh no," Tollin says, "I missed Zek! And on a week when I actually have something to deliver, too." After a moment he adds, "Not that I mind your taking it in his stead, of course. I just don't get to see him all that often, what with him always zipping about hither and yon." As he speaks, he grabs a metallic panel from his workbench and hands it to you.
> 
> "I borrowed this from Silaro Mako a while back. Very useful! Can you return it to him, along with my thanks? I think he's still up by the Fractured Wall studying Estian ruins or some such."

Complete the **RETRIEVE THE PACKAGE (TOLLIN LANG, SCULPTOR)** mission. Search the Artisan set for a Functional Replica and put it into play. If one cannot be found, search each ranger's deck, hand, and discard pile for one instead. Gain the **DELIVER THE PACKAGE (SILARO MAKO, ARCHAEOLOGIST)** mission and attach it to a copy of Functional Replica in play.
###### Travel to Fractured Wall and clear Silaro Mako with <span class="progress"></span> to deliver the package.

##### Elder Mora Orlin

> Smoke wreaths Elder Mora Orlin's head as she chews the end of her pipe, hands on her hips. "Don't put that there!" she growls at a passing artificer, arms full of tools. "That pile is for broken tools to be reclaimed. The finished tools go over there." She gestures with the end of her pipe, and then clamps it back firmly between her teeth with an audible click. The artificer whirls away towards the correct pile.
> 
> The Elder finally spots you. "What do you want? Keep standing there idle like that and I'm liable to put you to work!"
> 
> You explain that you're here to see if she needs anything delivered and she gives a grunt of acknowledgement. "There, on your left, you'll find a box full of boundary sensors," she says. You find it. "Grab a handful, and take them out to the Mound of the Navigator. Ben Amon just created a new cache out there last week and it needs more starting supplies. Now, go on! I've got plenty to do here, I've got no time to gab today."

Complete the **RETRIEVE THE PACKAGE (ELDER MORA ORLIN)** mission. Search the Explorer set for a Boundary Sensor and put it into play. If one cannot be found, search each ranger's deck, hand, and discard pile for one instead. Gain the **DELIVER THE PACKAGE (MOUND OF THE NAVIGATOR)** mission and attach it to a copy of Boundary Sensor in play.
###### Travel to Mound of the Navigator to deliver the package.
