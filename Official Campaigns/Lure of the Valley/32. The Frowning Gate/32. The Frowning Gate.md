**IF THIS IS YOUR FIRST VISIT, READ:**

> Although the land around the swamp is thick with trees overgrown with moss and vines, you recognize the infamous Frowning Gate as you approach. Ancient trees curl over the path to form a gnarled arch, heavy with drooping curtains of moss and choked with vines. Beyond the arch, the trail descends to the depths of the swamp.

**OTHERWISE, READ:**

> You find yourself once again faced with the overgrown trellis known as the Frowning Gate, which marks the easiest route into and out of the marsh. It has an ominous look to it, though wildlife here doesn't seem to mind at all.
