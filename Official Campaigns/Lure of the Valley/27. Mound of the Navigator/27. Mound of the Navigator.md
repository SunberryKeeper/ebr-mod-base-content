**IF THIS IS YOUR FIRST VISIT, READ:**

> From the pillar of rock known as the Mound of the Navigator, you can see for miles in all directions. Many centuries ago, the Estians cultivated an arboretum at the summit of the mound and carved the narrow switchback trails that lead there. As you approach, a flock of dark-feathered birds lifts to the sky, leading your eye out into the Valley and to the distant mountains beyond.

**OTHERWISE, READ:**

> You find yourself approaching the large rock column known as the Mound of the Navigator. If you're willing to brave its narrow switchbacks for a higher vantage point, you may be able to get a better look at your surroundings.
