**IF THIS IS YOUR FIRST VISIT, READ:**

> A reverent calm suffuses your being as you step into the Ancestor’s Grove. Each of the trees, from the smallest sapling to the towering giants at the grove’s center, has been planted over the final resting place of a loved one. You move with care through the grove. You see several people, some keeping to themselves, some speaking softly, some bearing gifts, all here to commune with those who have passed. You pause and give thought to your ancestors.

**OTHERWISE, READ:**

> Your pace slows as you find yourself once again traveling through the Ancestor's Grove, the final resting place for so many of the Valley's citizens. The mood here is always quiet, though not always solemn.
