**IF THIS IS YOUR FIRST VISIT, READ:**

> In a massive pothole carved over the centuries into the red-hued rock, someone has built a flourishing garden. Pipes, presumably carrying water, lead to and from the pothole and to smaller potholes on the periphery, each also growing carefully cultivated varieties of foliage. It's as if this sunkissed plateau housed a living lexicon of every plant in the Valley and beyond.

**OTHERWISE, READ:**

> Even though you've seen it before, this place still takes your breath away. This lovingly cultivated garden may be one of the most peaceful places the Valley has to offer.
