Each ranger should follow the rules outlined on pages 32-33 of the core set rulebook to build their ranger deck.

If it's your first time playing, here are some high-level deckbuilding tips:
- **Specialty Variety:** It's recommended that each player choose a different specialty, even if you have a ranger doubler. This provides a more varied multiplayer experience.
- **Aspect Balance:** The most important aspect is <span style="color: #c00b0b">Fitness</span>, followed by <span style="color: #b59e00">Spirit</span>. For your first deck, it's recommended to take at least a 2 in each of these aspects. Take your 1 in either <span style="color: #039b28">Awareness</span> or <span style="color: #6282c1">Focus</span>.
- **Balanced Approach Icons:** Each ranger will need to use all four approaches at some point during the game. Don't just min-max a single approach icon; seek a variety.
- **Double Approach Icons:** Cards with two of the same approach icon are more valuable than cards with many different approach icons.
- **Card Draw:** Card draw isn't as valuable in Earthborne Rangers as it is in other games—each card drawn shortens your day by one turn. The best way to add cards to your hand is by soothing fatigue; each deck will want a few ways to do this.
- **Scouting:** If you're playing at lower player counts, having cards that can scout the path deck and help you find a particular card is vital.

When each player is done building their ranger deck, proceed to [[1. Missions]].
