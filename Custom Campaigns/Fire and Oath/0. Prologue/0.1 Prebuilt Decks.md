Each ranger should choose one of the prebuilt decks. For the best experience and most up-to-date lists, see the [Premade Ranger Decks](https://earthbornegames.com/resources/ranger-decks/) page on the official website. Include the shown role card, the aspect card matching the listed ability values, and 2 copies of each listed card.

However, if you're offline, the lists are reproduced for you below. When each player has chosen one and assembled their ranger deck, proceed to entry [[1. Missions]].
