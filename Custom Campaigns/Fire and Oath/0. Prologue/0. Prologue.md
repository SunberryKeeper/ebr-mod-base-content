Fire and Oath is a prequel campaign to the core set campaign, Lure of the Valley. You can play Fire and Oath in place of Lure of the Valley's prologue; at the end of this campaign, you can continue into Lure of the Valley by reading its **1. Missions** entry. Throughout this campaign you'll modify your ranger deck; you can carry this deck forward as-is into Lure of the Valley.

Before this campaign starts, remove these cards from the Valley set:
- Tala the Red, Exile
- Ol' Bloody Clicker
- Quisi Vos, Rascal

Some of these cards are not used at all in this campaign. Others are brought into play only by the journal entries on other cards.

If you have the expansions Spire in Bloom or Shadow of the Storm, feel free to add the new cards to their respective decks. However, you should use the core set version of the Spire location (the one with journal entry 5 on it) and you should not use any of the new map elements from these expansions. The map you should use for this campaign is [[Map of the Valley (FaO).png]].

This prologue is not a substitute for the rulebook, nor does it teach you the fundaments of the game. However, it is intended to have a gradual difficulty ramp allowing new players to get their bearings and learn the game's core structure and strategies at their own pace. At least one player should be familiar with the game's basic rule framework before play begins.

When you encounter text like this, read it aloud:

> You've just made one of the biggest decisions of your life. You've decided to join the Rangers.
> 
> The Rangers are renowned throughout the Valley; in many ways they're the lifeblood of the community. They travel between villages, helping people, carrying goods to where they're needed most, and making safe the few roads that snake through the wilderness. If you're heading out into the wilds, you want a Ranger by your side.
> 
> When Calypsa, one of the lifelong cloak-wearers, came by your village and said she was looking for new recruits, you found yourself speaking before thinking. Since then, it's been a whirlwind of frantic packing, tearful goodbyes, gift-giving and gift-receiving. And then, impossibly, the freedom of the open wilderness stretching out before you.
> 
> Your new adventure is about to begin!

There are three options for creating your ranger deck:
- [[0.1 Prebuilt Decks]] — If you want to get started playing right away. You'll still be able to customize your ranger through playing the game. This is a good option if it's everyone's first time playing.
- [[0.2 Guided Character Creation]] — If you want to dive into your character's backstory more. This may take a whole game session of its own, but offers more customization. Recommended for groups who want to enjoy Earthborne Rangers like a role-playing game.
- [[0.3 Freeform Deckbuilding]] — If you are familiar with constructed-deck style card games and want total freedom to build your decks from scratch. Building decks for Earthborne Rangers can be tricky, even for players familiar with other card games, so it's recommended primarily for players who have already played before.

Select a page above to build your deck. Once you're done, it will direct you to [[1. Missions]] to start the campaign.
