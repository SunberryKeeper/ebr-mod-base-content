Familiar Ground fixes one of my pet peeves about the base game: you have to re-check the entry for each location every time you visit it, because sometimes there's some sort of interaction based on your game state. But most of the time, you end up just re-reading the same paragraph you read *every* time you pass through.

And some of them are *so long*.

I wanted to have something to read every time to re-set the scene, but not have that become a chore. So this mod introduces shortened descriptions for most locations that you can use whenever you revisit a location for the second (or third, or fourth) time.
## Compatibility

Only supports the base **Lure of the Valley** campaign for now, but I might add support for the other official content later.
## Special Rules

Most locations now have a section like this:

**IF THIS IS YOUR FIRST VISIT, READ:**

> The original text of the location.

You don't need to use any special state to track whether or not it's your first visit, just your own memory. If you're not sure, or just want a longer reintroduction to a location, feel free to re-read the "first visit" description. No game-flow changes have been made when re-visiting a location.
