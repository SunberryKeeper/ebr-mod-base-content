Help Zek Tsal, the stilthorse courier, make an important delivery in this highly replayable one-day mission. Card draws randomize the delivery path you'll be using each time you play, with 27 different fully-scripted story paths to play through.

Get out there, meet the folks of the Valley, and help make their day!
## Features

- 27 fully-scripted, replayable delivery routes.
- Difficulty varies. Play with either a starter deck or use your rewards!
- Lore-accurate and full of characterization for your favorite NPCs.
- Contains *light* spoilers for a small number of Reward cards and General set cards from Lure of the Valley, but no story spoilers.
